Package Overview

A paced first look at Dibang

This circuit starts and ends in Dibrugarh. Night one is Roing after the Dhola–Sadiya bridge. Day two is the long forest drive to Anini. Two nights then stay in Anini so Dri, Acheso and Mathun are not forced into a single exhausted afternoon. Night five returns to Roing before the plains drop.

Mathun Valley and some side valleys stay subject to road and permit conditions. Treat those stops as intended, not guaranteed.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 6

Scroll the journey. The car moves with you along the route — tap a day marker to jump ahead.

Day 01

Dibrugarh to Roing

Overnight · Roing

  • Pickup from Dibrugarh Airport / Railway Station.
  • En route stop at Bhupen Hazarika Setu (Dhola–Sadiya Bridge).
  • Check into the hotel in Roing. Dinner and overnight.
Day 02

Roing to Anini

Overnight · Anini

  • After breakfast, check out and drive to Anini (about 7–8 hours).
  • Mountain roads, rivers and forest valleys on the way.
  • Check into the hotel or homestay. Dinner and overnight in Anini.
Day 03

Explore Anini

Overnight · Anini

  • Dibang Valley views around town.
  • Acheso and Dri Valley.
  • Local Anini market.
  • Dinner and overnight in Anini.
Day 04

Anini local exploration

Overnight · Anini

  • Nearby stretches: Mipi and Emuli.
  • Mathun Valley — subject to road conditions and permits.
  • Riverside time and an unhurried return to Anini.
Day 05

Anini to Roing

Overnight · Roing

  • After breakfast, check out and drive back to Roing.
  • Check into the hotel and rest after the long corridor.
Day 06

Roing to Dibrugarh

Transfer · Dibrugarh Airport / Railway Station

  • After breakfast, check out.
  • Transfer to Dibrugarh Airport / Railway Station for departure.
