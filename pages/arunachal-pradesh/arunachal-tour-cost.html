Arunachal Pradesh Tour Cost

Package ranges, what sits inside the figure, and how to keep the week honest

Arunachal Travel Guide

The quote follows the circuit, not a single headline

A short Tawang week costs less than a remote run to Mechuka or Anini.

Most land packages cover stay, transport and listed sightseeing. Meals beyond breakfast and dinner, entry fees, some permits and personal spend sit outside unless the sheet says otherwise. Use the ranges below as planning bands, not a fixed menu.

A 6 night / 7 day western week

A common relaxed line is Guwahati → Shergaon → Sangti → Tawang → Jung → Mandala Top → Guwahati. Cost hangs on how many people share the cab.

2 travellers

  • About ₹35,000 – ₹55,000 per person

4 travellers

  • About ₹25,000 – ₹40,000 per person

Larger groups drop the per-person transport line further. Hotel category still moves the total.

What actually changes the price

Number of travellers

The vehicle is one of the largest lines. Two people in a private cab pay more each than four or six sharing the same car.

Stay category

Budget rooms and homestays, comfortable standard hotels, or premium / boutique stays where they exist. Remote valleys have fewer options, which can push the rate up.

Vehicle and route

Passenger count, road type, number of days and whether high-altitude excursions need a local vehicle all sit in this line.

Bum La

The excursion can add permit cost, a local arrangement and extra running. Confirm whether it is inside the package.

Season

Peak holidays, winter snow weeks, long weekends and festival dates tighten rooms. Book earlier for those windows.

Budget, standard and premium

BudgetHomestays, shared transport, popular circuits only
StandardComfortable rooms, private cab, listed sightseeing — the usual family and couple band
PremiumBetter rooms, more flexible days — relevant in Mechuka, Anini and Dibang

Arunachal can cost more than a short hill-station week because the drives are long, vehicles are specialised and remote rooms are scarce. A group still cuts the per-person figure more than almost anything else.

How to keep the week within budget

  • Travel as a group so the cab is shared
  • Choose one circuit — do not pay for Tawang plus Ziro plus Mechuka in one short trip
  • Stay off the peak holiday calendar when you can
  • Book early in winter, when rooms go first

Frequently asked

How much is a 7-day tour?

A standard private 6N/7D week often sits around ₹25,000 to ₹55,000 per person, driven mainly by group size and hotel band.

Is it expensive for a couple?

A private cab for two is a high per-person transport line. Four or more people usually drop that number sharply.

What does a Tawang tour cost?

Duration, hotels, start point and whether Bum La is included all matter. A typical private tour often lands around ₹20,000 to ₹50,000+ per person.

Is food included?

Check the sheet. Many of our packages include breakfast and dinner. Lunch is often left flexible on driving days.

Get a figure that matches your week

A short Tawang trip can sit in a moderate band. Mechuka or Anini needs more days and more road. The accurate quote comes after destinations, dates, headcount and hotel band are clear.
