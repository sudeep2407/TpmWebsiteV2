Best Time to Visit Arunachal Pradesh

Weather, seasons and which month matches the circuit you actually want

On this page

Seasons at a glance Winter Spring Summer Monsoon Autumn By destination FAQs
Arunachal Travel Guide

The month depends on the region

Tawang in snow is not the same holiday as Ziro in planting season, or Mechuka after the rains.

In general, October to April is the most reliable window for Tawang, Dirang, Shergaon and the other western highland stops. Ziro and Mechuka can still work outside that band if you accept greener, wetter roads.

Use this guide to match the season to the circuit — not to hunt for one perfect month for the whole state.

Seasons at a glance

SeasonMonthsTravel experience
WinterNovember to FebruarySnow, cold weather, high Himalayan landscapes
SpringMarch to AprilPleasant days and clearer mountain views
SummerMay to JuneGreen valleys, still comfortable at altitude
MonsoonJuly to SeptemberLush country, possible road holds
AutumnOctober to NovemberClear weather and the best overall mountain light
November to February

Winter — snow on the western highlands

This is the season most people picture for Tawang. Sela, Dirang, Shergaon, Sangti and Bum La sit in cold air; the higher ground can turn white. It suits snow, winter photographs and a quieter town — if you accept that Sela or Bum La can close for a day after a fall.

Best winter destinations

  • Tawang
  • Dirang, Shergaon, Sangti Valley
  • Sela Pass and Bum La Pass

Best for

  • Couples who want snow
  • Families comfortable with real cold
  • Winter photography
March to April

Spring — the balanced window

The hardest winter eases. Valleys start to green. Roads are usually kinder than January. This is a strong period for a first Arunachal week that is not built only around snow — Tawang still works, and so do Ziro and Mechuka.

Why spring works

  • Pleasant daytime temperatures
  • Comfortable sightseeing
  • Good light for photographs
  • Suitable for longer road trips
May to June

Summer — green valleys, still cool at height

The Indian plains are hot; Tawang, Dirang, Ziro and Mechuka stay comparatively comfortable. Valleys look their greenest. Weather can start to turn as monsoon approaches, so keep a buffer on the last driving day.

Summer suits family holidays, nature-led trips and unhurried valley time.

July to September

Monsoon — lush, and the least predictable

Forests and rivers look spectacular. It is also the season of landslides, holds and days that do not run as written. Higher ground can sit in cloud.

Worth it only if you are flexible. A first Tawang road trip is usually better outside peak monsoon.

October to November

Autumn — often the best all-round balance

Monsoon green is still on the hills. Rain eases. Ridges sharpen. October and November suit Tawang, Dirang, Shergaon, Sangti, Ziro and Mechuka — and they are the months many travellers should book first if they have no snow requirement.

Best time by destination

Tawang

  • Oct–Apr overall
  • Snow: December to February
  • Pleasant sightseeing: March to May
  • Clearest views: October and November

Ziro & Mechuka

  • Ziro: March–June and October–November
  • Paddy colour follows the farm calendar
  • Mechuka: March–June and October–November
  • Monsoon makes the long Mechuka road less reliable

Families usually do best in March–May and October–November. Honeymoons follow the same windows, or December–February if snow is the point.

Frequently asked

What is the best month overall?

October and November, or March and April.

When is snow most likely?

December to February, on the higher western ground — Sela, Bum La, Tawang. It varies year to year.

Can I go in December?

Yes, and it is a popular Tawang month. Expect real cold and possible day-long holds on the pass.

Is monsoon safe?

Travel is possible. Landslides happen. Keep the itinerary loose.
