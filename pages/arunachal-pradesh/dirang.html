Dirang

A river town that deserves more than a single night on the way to Tawang

West Kameng valley

The halt that should not be rushed

Dirang sits in a wider valley than Bomdila, with the river close, apple slopes in season and Sangti a short drive away. Many Tawang itineraries treat it as a bed between two long drives. It works better as a real stop: hot springs, a monastery morning, and a slow afternoon in the fields.

The altitude is kinder than Tawang, which makes Dirang useful for acclimatisation before the Sela climb. Two nights here change the feel of the whole western circuit.

Altitude ~1,500 m1–2 nights typicalOrchard & river base

Famous for

  • Sangti Valley walks and birdlife in the right months
  • Apple orchards around the town in harvest season
  • Dirang Dzong and the valley monastery
  • Hot springs used by local travellers after the mountain drive
  • A calmer overnight than jumping straight toward Sela

Best time to visit

  • March to April: spring colour, comfortable days, good roads toward the pass
  • October to November: clear skies and the easiest all-round window
  • December to February: cold nights, useful if Tawang is your next stop and Sela is open
  • June to September: rain on the approach roads — keep a buffer day
How the year feels

Dirang through the seasons

Spring

Blossom on the slopes and a green valley floor.

Summer

Full orchards, but cloud and rain on the higher road.

Autumn

The cleanest views toward the Tawang side.

Winter

A quiet, cold Dirang — still the better halt before a snowy pass.
