Roing

Foothill town, orange country and the climb toward Mayudia

Lower Dibang Valley

The practical gate to the Dibang side

Roing sits lower than Anini, in a belt of foothill forest and orange orchards, with the climb to Mayudia as the usual high day. Many eastern itineraries sleep here because the next road is long and because Mayudia, in winter, is one of the more reachable snow points in this part of the state.

Two nights work well: a town evening and a Mayudia day, with Anini only if you have more time. Summer rain changes the climb; winter frost makes the pass a different trip. Check the road the morning you go up.

Famous for

  • Mayudia Pass — a high, often misty saddle above town
  • Winter snow days that do not require the Tawang road
  • Orange orchards in the foothill belt
  • A practical overnight before the longer run toward Anini
  • Forest and river country of Lower Dibang

Best time to visit

  • October to April: the main window for Roing and Mayudia
  • December to February: the snow chance at Mayudia, roads permitting
  • March to April: milder foothill days
  • June to September: the climb is frequently interrupted
How the year feels

Roing through the seasons

Spring

Clearer foothills and a kinder Mayudia day.

Summer

Deep forest, wet roads, fewer reliable views.

Autumn

The easiest first visit to Lower Dibang.

Winter

Cold mornings and the best chance of snow at the pass.
