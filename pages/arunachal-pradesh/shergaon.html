Shergaon

A pine village off the main Tawang rush

Sherdukpen country

Quieter than the highway towns

Shergaon sits in West Kameng away from the Bomdila–Dirang trunk that most Tawang itineraries never leave. Pine slopes, orchard patches and Sherdukpen village life set the pace. It is not a checklist stop. It works when you have extra days and want a night that is not another hotel on the highway.

Reach it as a side valley from the western circuit, not as a rushed afternoon. One or two nights are enough. Combine it with Bomdila or Dirang rather than forcing it into a Tawang-only week.

West Kameng1–2 nightsSide-valley stay

Famous for

  • Sherdukpen village lanes and wooden houses
  • Pine forest walks above the settlement
  • Orchard country in the harvest months
  • A slower alternative to sleeping only in Bomdila
  • A sense of West Kameng beyond the Tawang highway

Best time to visit

  • March to May: comfortable village weather and open side roads
  • October to November: clear hills and quieter rooms
  • December to February: cold, still walkable on good days
  • June to September: rain on forest roads — keep plans loose
