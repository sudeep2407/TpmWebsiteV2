Package Overview

Tawang with the extra high valleys

This week keeps the classic Tawang core and adds the country most short plans skip: a Shergaon first night, Sangti Valley, a Mago–Chuna–Gongkar La day, Jung and a last highland night at Mandala Top. Guwahati is the start and the last two nights include a Kamakhya morning before you fly or take the train.

Bum La, Mago and Gongkar La stay weather- and permit-led. Jung Falls is covered on arrival only if daylight remains; otherwise it moves to the next morning.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 8

Scroll the journey. The car moves with you along the route — tap a day marker to jump ahead.

Day 01

Guwahati to Shergaon

Overnight · Shergaon

  • Pickup from Guwahati Airport / Railway Station.
  • Drive through the Assam plains and into the foothills toward Shergaon.
  • Check in. Evening at leisure in the pine village.
Day 02

Shergaon to Sangti Valley

Overnight · Sangti Valley

  • After breakfast, explore the Shergaon surroundings and drive toward Sangti.
  • Sangti Valley, riverside walks, village lanes and valley views.
  • Overnight in Sangti Valley.
Day 03

Sangti to Tawang via Sela

Overnight · Tawang

  • Dirang Monastery on the way out of the valley.
  • Sela Tunnel and Jaswant Singh War Memorial.
  • Continue to Tawang. Giant Buddha Statue if time remains.
  • Overnight in Tawang.
Day 04

Bum La & Sangetsar excursion

Overnight · Tawang

  • Full-day high-altitude excursion: Bum La Pass and Sangetsar Lake (Madhuri Lake).
  • Other permitted viewpoints and lakes en route.
  • Return to Tawang. Tawang War Memorial in the evening if time remains.
  • Subject to permit, weather and road access.
Day 05

Tawang, Mago & Jung

Overnight · Jung

  • Early Tawang Monastery and the Buddha Statue if not covered earlier.
  • Proceed toward Mago, Chuna Valley and Gongkar La Lake.
  • Continue to Jung. Nuranang / Jung Waterfall on arrival only if daylight remains.
  • Overnight in Jung.
Day 06

Jung, Sela & Mandala Top

Overnight · Mandala Top

  • Jung / Nuranang Waterfall in the morning if missed on Day 5.
  • Sela Pass and Sela Lake.
  • Continue to Mandala Top for overnight stay.
Day 07

Mandala Top to Guwahati

Overnight · Guwahati

  • Morning views around Mandala Top.
  • After breakfast, drive toward Guwahati.
  • Check into a Guwahati hotel or homestay and rest.
Day 08

Kamakhya & departure

Transfer · Guwahati Airport / Railway Station

  • Early morning visit to Kamakhya Temple.
  • After breakfast, check out and transfer for your onward journey.
