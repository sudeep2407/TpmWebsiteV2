Package Overview

The first-timer Tawang week

Five nights is the clean first Tawang circuit: a Dirang halt to break the climb, three nights in Tawang for the monastery and the Bum La day, then Bomdila on the way home. Pickup and drop are Guwahati Airport / Railway Station.

Bum La is built into this plan and still depends on permit, weather and road status on the day. Jung Falls and Sela Lake sit on the transfer days so the Tawang nights stay usable.

At a glance

  • Start & end: Guwahati Airport / Railway Station
  • Stay: Dirang 1N · Tawang 3N · Bomdila 1N
  • Ideal months: March – May, September – November
  • Inner Line Permit arranged
  • Private cab for pickup, drop and sightseeing

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 6

Scroll the journey. The car moves with you along the route — tap a day marker to jump ahead.

Day 01

Guwahati to Dirang

Overnight · Dirang

  • Pickup from Guwahati Airport / Railway Station and drive toward Arunachal.
  • Complete entry formalities and continue to Dirang.
  • Check into the homestay. Dinner and overnight in Dirang.
Day 02

Dirang to Tawang

Overnight · Tawang

  • After breakfast, check out and drive toward Tawang.
  • En route: Sela Tunnel, Jaswant Singh War Memorial and Jung Falls.
  • Check into the Tawang homestay. Dinner and overnight in Tawang.
Day 03

Tawang local sightseeing

Overnight · Tawang

  • Tawang Monastery on the ridge.
  • Tawang War Memorial.
  • Giant Buddha Statue.
  • Dinner at the homestay. Overnight in Tawang.
Day 04

Bum La Pass excursion

Overnight · Tawang

  • After breakfast, proceed for the Bum La day.
  • Bum La Pass, Pangyang Lake and Sangetsar Lake (Madhuri Lake).
  • The day’s plan stays subject to permit, weather and road access.
  • Return to Tawang for dinner and overnight.
Day 05

Tawang to Bomdila

Overnight · Bomdila

  • After breakfast, check out and drive toward Bomdila.
  • En route: Sela Pass, Sela Lake and Bomdila Monastery.
  • Check into the Bomdila homestay. Dinner and overnight.
Day 06

Bomdila to Guwahati

Transfer · Guwahati Airport / Railway Station

  • After breakfast, check out.
  • Transfer to Guwahati Airport / Railway Station for departure.
