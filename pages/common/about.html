About The Pine Mist

Thoughtfully planned journeys across India

Our Approach

Journeys designed around you

At The Pine Mist, we believe the best journeys aren’t simply about ticking destinations off a list. They are about travelling at the right pace, discovering places beyond the obvious, and having the freedom to enjoy a destination without worrying about every detail of the journey.

We are a travel company specialising in carefully planned holidays across the mountains, valleys, forests and cultural landscapes of India. From the snow-covered valleys of Kashmir and the dramatic landscapes of Ladakh to the forests of Meghalaya and the remote mountains of Arunachal Pradesh, we create thoughtfully planned journeys for travellers who want to experience India in a more meaningful way.

Travel is personal. Your journey should be too.

How we plan

There is no single definition of a perfect holiday

A family looking for a comfortable Himalayan vacation has very different needs from a couple planning a quiet mountain escape. An adventure traveller may want to explore remote regions, while another traveller may simply want beautiful scenery, comfortable stays and a relaxed itinerary.

That’s why we don’t believe in simply putting travellers into a standard package. We listen first. We understand your travel dates, preferences, budget, interests and expectations, and then build an itinerary around them.

What We Do

Customized holidays, planned with care

Whether you are planning a short Himalayan getaway or a multi-day journey through a remote region of Northeast India, the aim is simple: planning that is easy, and a journey that is comfortable.

Customized itineraries

Routes built around your dates, pace and the destinations that matter to you.

Stays & transport

Hotel and homestay arrangements with private transportation throughout the trip.

Sightseeing & experiences

Local days planned around weather, road time and what you actually want to see.

Family holidays

Comfortable pacing, sensible hotel changes and time that isn’t only spent in a car.

Couples & honeymoons

Quieter stays, scenic rooms and itineraries that leave room for the destination.

Groups & adventure

Shared circuits, mountain journeys and destination-specific planning for remote regions.

Destinations

Where we specialise

Our strongest expertise lies in India’s mountain and nature destinations. We plan holidays across Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Ladakh, Sikkim, Darjeeling, Meghalaya and Arunachal Pradesh, along with other destinations across the country.

We don’t look at a destination only as a collection of tourist attractions. Good travel planning requires understanding how a destination actually works — travel times, road conditions, seasonal access, weather, accommodation, local regulations, sightseeing hours and the pace of the itinerary.

We take these practical factors into account while designing trips. The result is an itinerary that is not only attractive on paper, but realistic and enjoyable when you actually travel it.

Our Philosophy

Go beyond the checklist

Some of the most memorable moments of a journey aren’t always the ones printed in a sightseeing brochure. It could be watching the first light reach a Himalayan valley, stopping at a small mountain village, sharing a conversation with a local, finding a quiet viewpoint away from the crowds, or simply having enough time to enjoy where you are.

We believe good travel leaves room for these moments. That’s why our approach is built around thoughtful planning rather than rushed sightseeing.

Who we plan for

For travellers who want to explore India differently

India offers an extraordinary variety of landscapes. The Himalayas alone stretch across regions that feel completely different from one another — from the alpine valleys of Kashmir to the high-altitude landscapes of Ladakh, the forests and rivers of Northeast India, and the culturally rich mountain communities of the Eastern Himalayas.

Our role is to help you discover these places in a way that suits your journey. Whether you are travelling with family, planning a honeymoon, organising a group holiday or looking for an adventure into the mountains, we can help you plan the journey from beginning to end.
