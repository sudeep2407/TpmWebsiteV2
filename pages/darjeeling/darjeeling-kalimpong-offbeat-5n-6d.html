Package Overview

Five village and town nights east of Darjeeling

This week stays off Mall Road. Night one is Kaffergaon after Panbu Dara. Night two is Rishop, with Lava Monastery, Lolegaon Eco Park and the pine forest. Kalimpong is the mid-week town night. Dawaipani covers Lamahatta, Takdah and Tinchuley. The last night is Sittong before the NJP drop.

Meals include breakfast, lunch, dinner and evening snacks on most nights. Entry tickets, rides and syndicate charges are extra.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 6

Day 01

NJP to Kaffergaon

Overnight · Kaffergaon

  • Pickup from NJP and transfer to Kaffergaon.
  • Visit Panbu Dara.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at Kaffergaon.
Day 02

Kaffergaon to Rishop

Overnight · Rishop

  • After breakfast, check out and drive to Rishop.
  • Lava Monastery, Lolegaon Eco Park, the Buddha statue and Rishop pine-forest walks.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at Rishop.
Day 03

Rishop to Kalimpong

Overnight · Kalimpong

  • After breakfast, check out and transfer to Kalimpong.
  • Kalimpong sightseeing: Deolo Park, Science City Park, Hanuman Tok and the Buddha statue.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner in Kalimpong.
Day 04

Kalimpong to Dawaipani

Overnight · Dawaipani

  • After breakfast, check out and drive to Dawaipani.
  • Lamahatta Park, Lovers’ Meet View Point, Gumbadara View Point and Peshok Tea Garden.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at Dawaipani.
Day 05

Dawaipani to Sittong

Overnight · Sittong

  • After breakfast, check out and transfer to Sittong.
  • Ahaldara View Point, Jogighat Bridge, Namthing Pokhri and Sittong Monastery.
  • Lunch and dinner at Sittong.
Day 06

Sittong to NJP

Transfer · NJP

  • After breakfast, check out from Sittong.
  • Drop at NJP for the onward journey.
