Darjeeling Town

The hill station on a ridge — tea gardens below, Kangchenjunga on a clear morning

Darjeeling district

The classic hill town of the eastern Himalaya

Darjeeling sits at about 2,045 metres on a ridge facing Kangchenjunga. Most visitors arrive from Bagdogra Airport or New Jalpaiguri after a three-to-four-hour climb. The town centres on Chowrasta and Mall Road — a walking square of cafés, shops and views. Below the ridge, tea bushes cover the slopes that gave the district its name.

Tiger Hill is the usual sunrise point. Batasia Loop, the Himalayan Railway (a UNESCO World Heritage line), Peace Pagoda, Observatory Hill and nearby tea estates fill a local day. The Toy Train runs on a limited schedule. October to December and March to May are the clearest windows.

Altitude ~2,045 mMall RoadTiger Hill

Famous for

  • Chowrasta and Mall Road, the pedestrian heart of town
  • Tiger Hill sunrise over Kangchenjunga
  • Tea estates on the surrounding slopes
  • The Darjeeling Himalayan Railway, the “Toy Train”
  • Peace Pagoda, Observatory Hill and Batasia Loop

Best time to visit

  • October to December: the clearest mountain views
  • March to May: mild days and spring blossom
  • January to February: cold, often still clear
  • June to September: monsoon mist and slower roads
How the year feels

Darjeeling Town through the seasons

Autumn

Sharp light on Kangchenjunga after the rains.

Winter

Cold nights and frequent clear mornings.

Spring

Pleasant walking weather around Mall Road.

Monsoon

Cloud over the range; the town stays open.
