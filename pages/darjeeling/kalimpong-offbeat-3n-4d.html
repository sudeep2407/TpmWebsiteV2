Package Overview

A short east-hills week without Darjeeling town

Three nights when you want Kalimpong country rather than Mall Road. Night one is Kaffergaon after Panbu Dara. Night two is Rishop for Lolegaon Eco Park, the Buddha statue, the pine forest and Lava Monastery. Night three is Ramdhura, with Kalimpong’s Deolo, Science City, Hanuman Tok and Buddha statue.

Pickup and drop are at NJP or Siliguri. Evening snacks are served with the main meals at the homestays. Entry and riding charges are extra.

Day 01

NJP to Kaffergaon

Overnight · Kaffergaon

  • Pickup from NJP and transfer to Kaffergaon.
  • Visit Panbu Dara.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at Kaffergaon.
Day 02

Kaffergaon to Rishop

Overnight · Rishop

  • After breakfast, check out and transfer to Rishop.
  • Lolegaon Eco Park, the Buddha statue, Rishop pine forest and Lava Monastery.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at the Rishop homestay.
Day 03

Rishop to Ramdhura

Overnight · Ramdhura

  • After breakfast, check out and transfer to Ramdhura.
  • Kalimpong sightseeing: Deolo Park, Science City Park, Hanuman Tok and the Buddha statue.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at the Ramdhura homestay.
Day 04

Ramdhura to NJP

Transfer · NJP

  • After breakfast, check out.
  • Drop at NJP for the onward journey.
