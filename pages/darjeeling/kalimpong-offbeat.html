Kalimpong & Offbeat

Kalimpong town plus the quieter hamlets — Lava, Lolegaon, Rishyap, Icchey Gaon, Tinchuley

Kalimpong district

A gentler town and the villages beyond it

Kalimpong sits at about 1,250 metres, lower and often milder than Darjeeling. Deolo Hill, Durpin Monastery, flower nurseries and a compact bazaar define the town. Many travellers add one night here on a Darjeeling–Sikkim circuit, or use it as a base for the eastern hamlets.

Offbeat stays nearby include Lava and Lolegaon (pine and canopy walks), Rishyap, Icchey Gaon and Tinchuley — small village ridges with homestays and valley views. October to May is the comfortable season across this belt.

Kalimpong ~1,250 mDeolo HillVillage stays

Famous for

  • Deolo Hill gardens and viewpoints
  • Durpin Monastery and the town nurseries
  • A milder climate than Darjeeling ridge
  • Lava, Lolegaon and Rishyap forest hamlets
  • Icchey Gaon and Tinchuley village stays

Best time to visit

  • October to May: the main window for town and hamlets
  • October to December: clear views from Deolo and the villages
  • March to May: flowers in nurseries and on the hills
  • Monsoon: Kalimpong stays reachable; village roads slow down
How the year feels

Kalimpong & Offbeat through the seasons

Autumn

Clear Deolo mornings and open village views.

Winter

Cool, dry days in town; cold nights in Lava.

Spring

Nurseries and hillside blossom.

Monsoon

Green hills and mist in the offbeat hamlets.
