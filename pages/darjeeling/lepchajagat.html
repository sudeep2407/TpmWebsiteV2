Lepchajagat

A small forest settlement above the mist, a short drive from Darjeeling

Near Ghoom

A quiet pine hamlet off the town ridge

Lepchajagat is a small settlement in the forest between Ghoom and Sukhia Pokhari, about 20 km from Darjeeling town. The name points to the Lepcha people of the eastern hills. Pine and oak stand close to the lodges. On a clear morning Kangchenjunga appears above the treeline.

People come here for silence, short forest walks and bird calls rather than a bazaar. It works as a one- or two-night alternative to staying only in Darjeeling town. October to May is the comfortable season; monsoon wraps the woods in cloud.

Forest hamlet~20 km from townKangchenjunga views

Famous for

  • Pine and oak forest around a handful of lodges
  • Clear-day views of Kangchenjunga
  • A quieter night than Mall Road
  • Birdwatching on short woodland paths
  • A useful halt between Darjeeling and the western hills

Best time to visit

  • October to December: clear mornings through the trees
  • March to May: mild days and spring forest
  • Winter: cold nights, often good visibility
  • Monsoon: deep mist; the hamlet stays open
