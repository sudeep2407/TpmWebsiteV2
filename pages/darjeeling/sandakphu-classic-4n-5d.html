Package Overview

The Singalila ridge in four nights

This is the high-ridge week, not a Mall Road circuit. Night one is Dhotre. Day two uses a separate cab from Manebhanjan up to Tonglu via Chitre, Ramdhura, Meghma and Meghma Monastery. Day three continues to Sandakphu through Tumling, Gairibas and Kalipokhri, with Ahal for sunset when the weather holds. Night four is Simana; Mirik sightseeing sits on the drive back to NJP.

National-park entry and NJP syndicate charges are extra. The Manebhanjan–Tonglu and return legs use a separate hill cab, included in cab charges. The ridge is best in April–May and October–November.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 5

Day 01

NJP to Dhotre

Overnight · Dhotre

  • Pickup from NJP and transfer to Dhotre.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at Dhotre.
  • Overnight at Dhotre.
Day 02

Dhotre to Tonglu via Manebhanjan

Overnight · Tonglu

  • Early breakfast and checkout by 8 am.
  • Drive to Manebhanjan, then continue in a separate cab toward Tonglu.
  • Stops typically include Chitre, Ramdhura, Meghma, Meghma Monastery and Tonglu.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at Tonglu.
Day 03

Tonglu to Sandakphu

Overnight · Sandakphu

  • After breakfast, continue along the ridge to Sandakphu.
  • Visit Tumling, Gairibas and Kalipokhri.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at Sandakphu.
  • After lunch, Ahal for sunset when the sky is clear.
Day 04

Sandakphu to Simana

Overnight · Simana

  • After breakfast, check out and drive down to Manebhanjan.
  • Change to the separate cab and continue to Simana.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at Simana.
Day 05

Simana to NJP via Mirik

Transfer · NJP

  • After breakfast, check out from Simana.
  • Mirik sightseeing: Nepal border, Pashupati Market, Mirik Lake, Mirik Tea Garden, Gopal Dhara Tea Estate and Simana View Point.
  • Drop at NJP.
