Manali Kasol Relaxed 5 Nights 6 Days

Solang and Old Manali, then two slower nights on the Parvati River

5 Nights 6 Days Manali + Kasol Breakfast & Dinner Private Transfers
Request This Package WhatsApp Quote
Package Overview

Two valleys, fewer hotel changes

Three nights in Manali cover the town, Old Manali and a Solang day. Two nights in Kasol then slow the week down — river cafés, Manikaran, and an optional walk toward Chalal or Kheerganga if time and trail conditions allow.

Pickup is from Delhi or Chandigarh. Solang adventure activities, Rohtang and the longer Kheerganga trek sit outside the land package and depend on permits, weather and extra charges.

Relaxed pace Couples & friends Parvati Valley

At a glance

  • Start & end: Delhi / Chandigarh Airport / Railway Station
  • Stay mix: 3 nights Manali + 2 nights Kasol
  • Ideal months: March – June, September – November
  • Private vehicle between the two valleys
  • Solang, Rohtang and Kheerganga extra / weather-led
Package Highlights

What this circuit includes

3 + 2 night stays

Three nights in Manali and two nights in Kasol — one transfer between valleys.

Breakfast & dinner

Daily breakfast and dinner at the stay. Café lunches in Kasol stay flexible.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 6

Scroll the journey. The car moves with you along the route — tap a day marker to jump ahead.

Day 01

Arrival in Manali

Overnight · Manali

  • Pickup from Delhi / Chandigarh and drive to Manali.
  • Arrive in Manali and check into your hotel.
  • Evening stroll at Mall Road and Van Vihar.
  • Overnight stay in Manali.
Day 02

Manali Local Sightseeing

Overnight · Manali

  • Visit Hadimba Temple, Club House, Vashisht Hot Springs and the Tibetan Monastery.
  • Explore Old Manali cafés and riverside walks.
  • Overnight stay in Manali.
Day 03

Solang Valley / Rohtang Pass Excursion

Overnight · Manali

  • Excursion to Solang Valley for adventure activities (paragliding, zorbing, skiing in winter — extra).
  • Optional trip to Rohtang Pass (subject to permits and weather, extra cost).
  • Return to Manali for overnight stay.
Day 04

Manali – Kasol

Overnight · Kasol

  • Drive to Kasol (approximately 2–3 hours).
  • Explore the Parvati River, local cafés and Israeli cuisine.
  • Evening leisure in the valley.
  • Overnight stay in Kasol.
Day 05

Kasol – Manikaran Excursion

Overnight · Kasol

  • Visit Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara and the hot springs.
  • Optional trek to Chalal Village or Kheerganga (if time and trail conditions permit — extra).
  • Return to Kasol for overnight stay.
Day 06

Departure

Transfer · Delhi / Chandigarh Airport / Railway Station

  • Breakfast and check-out.
  • Drive back to Delhi / Chandigarh for onward journey.
  • Drop at Airport / Railway Station for departure.
Good to Know

Inclusions & exclusions

Included

  • 3 nights stay in Manali and 2 nights stay in Kasol
  • Daily breakfast and dinner
  • Private cab for pickup drop and inter-city drives
  • Guided sightseeing as listed in the itinerary
  • Driver allowances, tolls and parking

Not included

  • Airfare or train tickets to Delhi / Chandigarh
  • Lunches, café meals, personal shopping and tips
  • Solang adventure activities (paragliding, zorbing, skiing)
  • Rohtang Pass permit, union charges and extra vehicle if required
  • Kheerganga / Chalal guide and trek charges
  • Entry fees where levied by local authorities
