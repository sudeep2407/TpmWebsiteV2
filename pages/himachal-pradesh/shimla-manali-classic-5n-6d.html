5 Nights 6 Days Shimla + Manali Breakfast & Dinner Private Transfers

Package Overview

The classic hill-station pairing

This is the circuit most first-time Himachal travellers start with: two nights in Shimla for the Ridge, Mall Road and a Kufri morning, then the long valley drive into Manali for town sights, Solang and an optional Rohtang day.

Pickup is from Delhi or Chandigarh. Hotels can be set at a comfortable Standard or Deluxe starting point; Premium stays are available on request. Adventure activities at Solang and the Rohtang excursion run on permits, weather and extra charges — they are not folded into the land package by default.

Package Highlights

What this circuit includes

2 + 3 night stays

Two nights in Shimla and three nights in Manali, so the long transfer happens only once.

Breakfast & dinner

Daily breakfast and dinner at the stay. Lunches stay flexible on the Shimla–Manali driving day.

Private transfers

Pickup, the hill drive and guided sightseeing in a dedicated vehicle for your group.

Two hill towns

Ridge evenings in Shimla and Solang or Rohtang time in Manali — paced rather than packed.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 6

Scroll the journey. The car moves with you along the route — tap a day marker to jump ahead.

Day 01

Delhi / Chandigarh – Shimla

Overnight · Shimla

  • Pick-up from Delhi / Chandigarh and drive to Shimla.
  • Check into the hotel.
  • Evening stroll at Mall Road and The Ridge.
  • Overnight stay in Shimla.
Day 02

Shimla – Kufri Excursion

Overnight · Shimla

  • Visit Kufri for panoramic Himalayan views.
  • Explore Green Valley, Indira Tourist Park and the Mini Zoo.
  • Evening free for shopping at Lakkar Bazaar.
  • Overnight stay in Shimla.
Day 03

Shimla – Manali

Overnight · Manali

  • Scenic drive to Manali (approximately 7–8 hours).
  • En route visit Kullu Valley and enjoy river rafting (optional, extra).
  • Check into the hotel in Manali.
  • Overnight stay in Manali.
Day 04

Manali Local Sightseeing

Overnight · Manali

  • Visit Hadimba Temple, Club House, Vashisht Hot Springs and the Tibetan Monastery.
  • Evening leisure at Mall Road.
  • Overnight stay in Manali.
Day 05

Solang Valley / Rohtang Pass Excursion

Overnight · Manali

  • Excursion to Solang Valley for adventure activities (paragliding, zorbing, skiing in winter — extra).
  • Optional trip to Rohtang Pass (subject to permits and weather, extra cost).
  • Return to Manali for overnight stay.
Day 06

Manali – Delhi / Chandigarh Departure

Transfer · Delhi / Chandigarh Airport / Railway Station

  • Drive back to Delhi / Chandigarh.
  • Drop at Airport / Railway Station for onward journey.
Good to Know

Inclusions & exclusions

Included

  • 2 nights stay in Shimla and 3 nights stay in Manali
  • Daily breakfast and dinner
  • Private cab for pickup drop and inter-city drives
  • Guided sightseeing as listed in the itinerary
  • Driver allowances, tolls and parking

Not included

  • Airfare or train tickets to Delhi / Chandigarh
  • Lunches, personal shopping and tips
  • Kufri local activity charges and park tickets
  • Kullu river rafting
  • Solang adventure activities (paragliding, zorbing, skiing)
  • Rohtang Pass permit, union charges and extra vehicle if required
  • Entry fees where levied by local authorities
