Shimla

Ridge walks, cedar shade and the unhurried heart of Himachal’s first hill station

Himachal’s Hill Capital

A town built for walking, not driving

Shimla sits on a long, forested ridge above the Shivaliks, still carrying the layout of the old summer seat — a pedestrian Mall, a church square on the Ridge, and cedar-lined slopes that drop toward Cart Road. Most Himachal family circuits begin here because the town is easy to reach from Chandigarh and because two nights are enough to feel the place without treating it as a one-evening stop.

A good Shimla stay mixes an early Mall walk before the shops fill, a quiet hour at Christ Church, and a half-day toward Kufri or Naldehra. The UNESCO toy train from Kalka is worth building into arrival if your dates allow the slower approach.

Altitude ~2,205 m2 nights typicalFamily-friendly base

Famous for

  • The Ridge and pedestrian Mall Road, with Christ Church as the town’s landmark
  • Kalka–Shimla toy train through tunnels and hillside stations
  • Jakhu Temple and the forested walk to Shimla’s highest spur
  • Kufri for winter snow and short hillside outings
  • Viceregal Lodge gardens and cedar walks above the old town

Best time to visit

  • March to April: warming days, blossom on the slopes and thinner crowds than peak summer
  • May to June: the classic plains-escape window — busy hotels, long evenings on the Mall
  • September to November: the clearest light of the year, apple season in the nearby orchards
  • December to February: frost on the Ridge and snow at Kufri — dress for cold evenings
How the year feels

Shimla through the seasons

Spring

The ridge greens quickly. Pleasant walking weather and the first colour in the gardens.

Summer

Full hill-station mood — café evenings, toy-train arrivals and a packed Mall after dusk.

Autumn

Sharp views after the rains, gold in the orchards and the quietest comfortable weeks.

Winter

Ice on the Ridge some mornings, snow outings to Kufri and a slower, jacket-weather town.
