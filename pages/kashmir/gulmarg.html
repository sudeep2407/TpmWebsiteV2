Gulmarg

A high bowl of meadow and pine — cable cars in summer, powder in winter

Meadow of Flowers

Kashmir’s most dramatic day out of Srinagar

Gulmarg sits about two hours west of Srinagar, a wide alpine clearing ringed by fir and the Apharwat ridge. The name hints at summer — a meadow that fills with wild colour — but the place is just as well known for its winter coat. The two-stage gondola lifts visitors first to Kongdoori and then, weather allowing, toward Apharwat, where the view opens across the Pir Panjal.

Families often treat Gulmarg as a long day trip. Couples and snow-seekers usually stay a night so they can take the first cable-car window and walk the meadow before the afternoon rush.

Altitude ~2,650 m ~52 km from Srinagar 1 night or a full day

Famous for

  • The two-stage gondola climbing toward Apharwat Peak
  • One of Asia’s most accessible lift-served ski bowls
  • Summer meadow walks, ponies and picnic clearings
  • A high golf green that sits among the firs in the warmer months
  • Snow play at Kongdoori even when the upper stage is closed

Best time to visit

  • December to February: deepest snow, skiing and a true winter landscape
  • March to April: lingering snow on the upper slopes, quieter trails below
  • May to June: wildflowers, open walking and the clearest family weather
  • September to October: sharp views, thinner crowds, cool nights
How the year feels

Gulmarg through the seasons

Winter

Powder on Apharwat, sledges at the first station and the gondola operating around weather windows.

Spring

Snow retreats from the bowl while the ridge still holds white — a mixed landscape many travellers love.

Summer

Green underfoot, long walks and picnic weather. Carry a jacket for the upper gondola stage.

Autumn

The air clears. Photographs of the Pir Panjal are often at their best before the first serious snow.
