Package Overview

A slower valley week built around two people

This circuit is paced for a honeymoon, not a checklist. You begin on a Dal Lake houseboat with a decorated shikara evening, keep a full day in Srinagar for gardens and photographs, then stay two nights by the Lidder in Pahalgam. Sonmarg and Gulmarg each get their own overnight — mountain-view rooms and a last-evening bonfire — instead of being treated as day trips from the city.

Room décor, private moments and the houseboat dinner are part of the plan. Hotel category can move between Deluxe and Premium when we quote; the shape of the week stays the same.

Luxury houseboat night Two nights in Pahalgam Overnight Sonmarg & Gulmarg

At a glance

  • Start & end: Srinagar Airport / Railway Station
  • Srinagar 2N · Pahalgam 2N · Sonmarg 1N · Gulmarg 1N
  • Ideal months: April – October; snow weeks Dec – Feb
  • Candlelight dinner, décor and private photoshoot
  • Private vehicle for the full circuit
Honeymoon Highlights

What makes this week different

Houseboat & candlelight

A luxury Dal Lake stay with a decorated shikara, rose petals, kahwa and dinner onboard on night one.

Private experiences

Romantic room décor, a viewpoint photoshoot and a picnic setup with Kashmiri dishes in Pahalgam.

Scenic valleys

Two nights by the Lidder, a glacier evening in Sonmarg and a gondola day that ends in Gulmarg.

Shikara, gardens, gondola

The three signature Kashmir moments sit on separate days so none of them feel squeezed.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 7

Scroll the journey. The car moves with you along the route — tap a day marker to jump ahead.

Day 01

Arrival in Srinagar

Overnight · Luxury houseboat on Dal Lake

  • Warm welcome at Srinagar Airport.
  • Transfer to a luxury houseboat on Dal Lake.
  • Evening Shikara ride with a decorated boat, rose petals and Kashmiri kahwa.
  • Candlelight dinner onboard.
Day 02

Srinagar Leisure & Romance

Overnight · Srinagar, romantic room décor

  • Morning visit to the Mughal Gardens — Shalimar Bagh and Nishat Bagh.
  • Private photoshoot at the Shankaracharya Temple viewpoint.
  • Stroll through Lal Chowk for souvenirs.
  • Overnight in Srinagar with romantic room décor.
Day 03

Pahalgam

Overnight · Riverside resort, Pahalgam

  • Scenic drive to Pahalgam (approximately 4 hours).
  • En route: saffron fields (seasonal) and the Awantipora ruins.
  • Evening walk along the Lidder River.
  • Overnight in a riverside resort.
Day 04

Pahalgam Exploration

Overnight · Pahalgam

  • Excursion to Betaab Valley and Aru Valley.
  • Horse ride or nature walk through the pine forests.
  • Picnic setup with Kashmiri delicacies.
  • Overnight stay in Pahalgam.
Day 05

Sonmarg

Overnight · Sonmarg, mountain-view room

  • Drive to Sonmarg (approximately 3 hours).
  • Pony ride toward Thajiwas Glacier.
  • Riverside stroll along the Sindh River.
  • Overnight stay in Sonmarg in a mountain-view room.
Day 06

Gulmarg

Overnight · Gulmarg resort

  • Drive to Gulmarg (approximately 2 hours).
  • Ride the Gulmarg Gondola toward Apharwat Peak (weather permitting).
  • Skiing in winter, or meadow walks in summer.
  • Romantic bonfire evening at the resort.
  • Overnight stay in Gulmarg.
Day 07

Departure

Transfer · Srinagar Airport / Railway Station

  • Return drive to Srinagar.
  • Visit Hazratbal Shrine, or keep the morning for last-minute shopping.
  • Transfer to Srinagar Airport / Railway Station for departure.
Good to Know

Inclusions & exclusions

  • Luxury houseboat night with candlelight dinner
  • Hotel stays as per the night plan, with romantic room décor
  • Daily breakfast and dinner
  • Private cab for pickup drop and inter-city drives
  • Complimentary Shikhara ride at Dal Lake

Not included

  • Lunches, personal shopping and tips
