Best Time to Visit Ladakh

The month depends on whether you want open circuits, fewer people, or winter snow

On this page

Months at a glance May to September October Winter By travel style Altitude first
Ladakh Travel Guide

Ladakh does not look the same all year

The landscape changes with the season. The right month depends on what you want from the week.

For most travellers, May to September is the practical window. Leh, Nubra and Pangong are generally reachable, and road days are workable. Outside that band you can still go — for snow, quieter streets or photographs — if you accept what the season will not give you.

May to September — the working holiday window

This is when a conventional Leh–Nubra–Pangong week is designed to run. Monasteries around Leh, Khardung La and the lakes can sit in one itinerary. Road tours have the most flexibility.

May

The season begins. Higher ground can still hold snow. Leh starts to fill. Good if you want fewer people, softer prices than July, snow on the walls and quieter town evenings. Check the pass the morning you leave.

June

One of the best all-round months. Weather suits sightseeing. The classic loop and longer weeks toward Tso Moriri or a Srinagar / Manali approach all become more realistic.

July and August

Peak demand — school holidays, full rooms, busy shores and markets. Book hotels and cabs early.

September

The rush eases. The land is still spectacular. Strong for couples, photographs, bikes and anyone who wants the circuit without July’s crowd.

Is October workable?

Leh can still be visited. Nights drop fast. Some seasonal stays wind down. If the week must include remote lakes and long side valleys, summer is simpler. If you like cold and empty roads, October is a different Ladakh.

November to February

Winter is another destination. Temperatures fall well below freezing. Many standard circuits stop being casual. It suits snow photographs, a quiet Leh, frozen-river country and winter expeditions — including Chadar when conditions and rules allow. It needs more preparation than a summer package.

Best time by travel style

  • June to September

Couples

  • May–June or September

  • May, June and September · bikes June–September

Snow

  • November to February

There is no automatic “worst” month. There is only a season that does not match what you booked the week for.

Give the body two nights in Leh

Leh is already high. Pangong and Tso Moriri sit higher. Do not judge a plan by how many names it lists on day one. A comfortable itinerary is worth more than a rushed one.

Best overall: June to September. Fewer people and better value: May and September. Snow: December to February, if you are ready for the cold.
