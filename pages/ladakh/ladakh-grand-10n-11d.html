Package Overview

The full high-plateau road

Ten nights is the week that can honestly hold both lakes and Hanle. Sham and two Nubra nights with Turtuk come first. Pangong, then Hanle, then Tso Moriri, then a long return to Leh and a buffer day before the airport. That last Leh night is not padding — the eastern circuit runs late when weather moves.

This is not a 6-day trip stretched. If you only have a week, pick Highlights or Explorer instead of cutting nights off this route.

At a glance

  • Start & end: Leh Airport
  • Stay: Leh 5N · Nubra 2N · Pangong 1N · Hanle 1N · Tso Moriri 1N
  • Ideal months: June – September
  • Inner Line Permit arranged
  • Private cab for the full expedition
Package Highlights

What this circuit includes

Leh and Sham first

Three easy days before Khardung La.

Nubra and Turtuk

Two valley nights, not a single exhausted afternoon.

Pangong then Tso Moriri

One night at each lake, with Hanle between them.

Hanle and a buffer day

Dark sky if the night is clear; a spare Leh day after the long east.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 11

Scroll the journey. The car moves with you along the route — tap a day marker to jump ahead.

Day 01

Arrival in Leh — complete rest

Overnight · Leh

  • Arrive at Leh Airport and transfer to the hotel.
  • The entire day is reserved for acclimatisation.
Day 02

Leh local sightseeing

Overnight · Leh

  • Shanti Stupa, Leh Palace, Sankar Monastery and Leh Market.
  • Return to the hotel and rest.
Day 03

Sham Valley excursion

Overnight · Leh

  • Pathar Sahib, Magnetic Hill, Nimmu and the confluence.
  • Basgo and Alchi. Return to Leh.
Day 04

Leh to Nubra via Khardung La

Overnight · Nubra Valley

  • Khardung La into Nubra.
  • Diskit Monastery and the Maitreya.
  • Hunder sand dunes.
Day 05

Nubra to Turtuk and back

Overnight · Nubra Valley

  • Turtuk village and Balti lanes.
  • Return to Nubra for overnight.
Day 06

Nubra to Pangong via Shyok

Overnight · Pangong Lake

  • Shyok Valley toward Pangong.
  • Remainder of the day beside the lake.
Day 07

Pangong to Hanle

Overnight · Hanle

  • Eastern Ladakh toward Hanle.
  • Check in. Stargazing if conditions and access allow.
Day 08

Hanle to Tso Moriri

Overnight · Tso Moriri

  • Continue across remote Changthang country.
  • Arrive Tso Moriri and check in.
  • Evening on a quieter shore than Pangong.
Day 09

Tso Moriri to Leh

Overnight · Leh

  • Morning at the lake.
  • Long high-altitude drive back to Leh.
  • Check in. Rest.
Day 10

Leh leisure / buffer day

Overnight · Leh

  • A deliberately light day after the eastern circuit.
  • Market, souvenirs or simply the hotel.
  • Useful if an earlier road day has already slipped.
Day 11

Leh departure

Transfer · Leh Airport

  • After breakfast, transfer to Leh Airport.
Good to Know

Inclusions & exclusions

  • Daily breakfast and dinner
