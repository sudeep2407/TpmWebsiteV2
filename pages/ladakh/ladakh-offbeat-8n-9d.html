Package Overview

The classic loop, then east to Hanle

Eight nights keeps Sham, two Nubra nights with Turtuk, Pangong via Shyok — and then leaves the lake for Hanle instead of returning straight to Leh. Hanle is a long, thin-air drive onto the Changthang. The night sky is the reason. It is not a first-week add-on unless the days are already here.

Day eight is a long return to Leh. Do not book a tight early flight on day nine if weather has already moved the clock.

Day 01

Arrival in Leh — acclimatisation

Overnight · Leh

  • Arrive in Leh and transfer to the hotel.
  • Rest and acclimatise throughout the day.
Day 02

Leh local sightseeing

Overnight · Leh

  • Shanti Stupa, Leh Palace, Sankar Monastery and Leh Market.
  • Keep the evening relaxed.
Day 03

Sham Valley excursion

Overnight · Leh

  • Pathar Sahib, Magnetic Hill, Nimmu and the confluence.
  • Basgo and Alchi. Return to Leh.
Day 04

Leh to Nubra via Khardung La

Overnight · Nubra Valley

  • Khardung La into Nubra.
  • Diskit Monastery and Hunder dunes.
Day 05

Nubra to Turtuk and back

Overnight · Nubra Valley

  • Drive to Turtuk after breakfast.
  • Balti village walk. Return to Nubra in the evening.
Day 06

Nubra to Pangong via Shyok

Overnight · Pangong Lake

  • Shyok Valley toward Pangong.
  • Evening beside the lake.
Day 07

Pangong to Hanle

Overnight · Hanle

  • Leave Pangong after breakfast for eastern Ladakh.
  • Long, thinly inhabited high country.
  • Check in. Night sky if conditions and local access allow.
Day 08

Hanle to Leh

Overnight · Leh

  • A quiet Hanle morning.
  • Long drive back to Leh.
  • Evening free in town.
Day 09

Leh departure

Transfer · Leh Airport

  • After breakfast, transfer to Leh Airport.
