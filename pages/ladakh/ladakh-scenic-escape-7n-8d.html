Ladakh Scenic Escape 7 Nights 8 Days

Leh → Sham → Nubra → Turtuk → Pangong → Leh — the classic loop plus a Balti village day

Package Overview

Nubra with time for Turtuk

Seven nights keeps the Explorer shape and adds a full Turtuk day from a second Nubra night. Turtuk is a Balti village at the far end of the valley — lanes, orchards and a different language belt. It is not a halt you squeeze after Khardung La on the same afternoon.

Three Leh nights sit before the pass (arrival, town, Sham). Pangong is still one night. Return via Chang La.

At a glance

  • Start & end: Leh Airport
  • Stay: Leh 4N · Nubra 2N · Pangong 1N
  • Ideal months: June – September
  • Inner Line Permit arranged
  • Private cab for the full loop

Diskit and Hunder on arrival; Turtuk the next day.

Turtuk village

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 8

Scroll the journey. The car moves with you along the route — tap a day marker to jump ahead.

Day 01

Arrival in Leh

Overnight · Leh

  • Arrive at Leh Airport and transfer to the hotel.
  • Rest for the day and acclimatise.
Day 02

Leh local sightseeing

Overnight · Leh

  • Shanti Stupa, Leh Palace and Sankar Monastery.
  • Leh Market.
  • Return to the hotel and rest.
Day 03

Sham Valley excursion

Overnight · Leh

  • Pathar Sahib, Magnetic Hill and Nimmu.
  • Indus–Zanskar confluence.
  • Basgo and Alchi Monastery.
  • Return to Leh by evening.
Day 04

Leh to Nubra via Khardung La

Overnight · Nubra Valley

  • Cross Khardung La into Nubra.
  • Diskit Monastery and the giant Buddha.
  • Hunder sand dunes. Overnight in Nubra.
Day 05

Nubra to Turtuk and back

Overnight · Nubra Valley

  • After breakfast, drive to Turtuk.
  • Walk the village — Balti houses, narrow lanes, orchard edges.
  • Return to the Nubra stay in the evening.
Day 06

Nubra to Pangong via Shyok

Overnight · Pangong Lake

  • Shyok route toward Pangong.
  • Check in. Evening beside the lake.
Day 07

Pangong to Leh via Chang La

Overnight · Leh

  • Morning at the lake.
  • Drive to Leh across Chang La.
  • Evening at leisure in town.
Day 08

Leh departure

Transfer · Leh Airport

  • After breakfast, transfer to Leh Airport.
