Ladakh Tour Plan

The order of places matters as much as the list — altitude first, then the lakes

On this page

How many days 6N / 7D loop 7N to 10D Road approaches What to include Three planning rules
Ladakh Travel Guide

Do not just join dots on a map

High altitude, long drives and roads that close mean the sequence matters.

A good week lets the body settle in Leh before Nubra or Pangong. For most first visits, Leh, Nubra and Pangong is the right introduction. Extra days add Sham, Turtuk, Tso Moriri or a Srinagar / Manali approach — not all of them in six nights.

How many days are enough?

DurationWhat fits
4–5 daysShort Leh and nearby sights — tight for both lakes
6–7 daysLeh + Nubra + Pangong
7–8 daysThe same loop with more air
8–9 daysAdd Tso Moriri or a fuller Sham / Turtuk day
9–10+ daysFuller circuit or a road-in / road-out week

First visit: plan for at least 6 nights / 7 days. See our Explorer and Highlights packages.

A practical 6 night / 7 day shape

Day 1Arrive Leh. Rest. No pass.
Day 2Leh town — palace, stupa, market
Day 3Khardung La → Nubra (Diskit, Hunder)
Day 4Nubra → Pangong via Shyok
Day 5Pangong → Leh via Chang La
Day 6Sham Valley / monastery day
Day 7Leh Airport

Nubra is the green trough after the pass — Diskit, Hunder dunes, double-hump camels. Pangong sits around 4,350 m. Do not put either on the afternoon you land.

When you have more nights

7 nights / 8 days

Move Sham before Nubra and keep an extra Nubra or Leh night so every day is not a transfer. Our Scenic Escape uses the extra night for Turtuk.

8–9 nights

Room for Tso Moriri or Hanle — not both unless the week is already at ten nights. Offbeat turns east after Pangong.

10+ nights

The Grand circuit holds both lakes, Hanle and a buffer night in Leh. That last Leh day is for weather slip, not shopping only.

Entering by road

Srinagar → Sonamarg → Drass → Kargil → Lamayuru → Leh climbs gradually and makes Kargil a real night. Manali → Leh is a different high-altitude road, only when the season allows. Both are approaches, not replacements for two rest nights in Leh before Nubra.

What belongs on the plan

Always, on a first week

  • Leh — palace, stupa, market, the acclimatisation base
  • Nubra — Diskit and Hunder after Khardung La
  • Pangong — one night if dawn matters

When the days exist

  • Sham Valley — Pathar Sahib, confluence, Alchi
  • Lamayuru — gompa and the eroded moonscape
  • Tso Moriri — quieter lake on a longer week

Three rules

Acclimatise first. The airport is not a launch pad for Chang La.

Do not stack six long drives. Six names in six days look complete and feel like a commute.

Leave slack. Weather and roads move the clock. A spare Leh night saves the flight.

First-timers: 6N/7D or 7N/8D. Season notes live in Best Time to Visit Ladakh. Budget notes in Ladakh Tour Cost.
