Best Time to Visit Meghalaya

October to April for roads and clear water — June to September if the falls are the reason

On this page

Seasons at a glance October to April Monsoon By destination By travel style
Meghalaya Travel Guide

The month decides whether you get roads or water

Meghalaya does not offer one “perfect week.” The monsoon fills the cliffs. Winter clears the roads and the Umngot.

For most travellers the working window is October to April. If Nohkalikai and Seven Sisters at full voice are the point, consider June to September — and leave slack in the itinerary.

When is the best time?

SeasonMonthsBest for
SpringMarch – AprilSightseeing, green hills, easier roads
Pre-monsoonMayFalls starting to rise; weather turning
MonsoonJune – SeptemberMaximum water, mist, green — slower drives
AutumnOctober – NovemberGreen after rain, better road trips
WinterDecember – FebruaryCool, drier, thinner falls, clearer Dawki

Best overall / couples / families / road trips: October–April. Best for waterfalls: June–September. Best balance of water and travel: October.

October to April

October–November is the balanced week: green after the rain, roads more honest, photography still good. December–February is cool and dry — the classic first-timer window, with slimmer falls and a better chance of clear Umngot. March–April stays comfortable for the Shillong–Sohra–Dawki–Mawlynnong loop. May is a hinge month: more water on the cliffs, more humidity, less certainty.

June to September

This is when Sohra and Mawsynram earn their reputation. Nohkalikai, Seven Sisters, Wei Sawdong and Dainthlen run hard. Cloud sits in the folds. The same rain that makes the photograph can close a stair or double a drive.

Come for water and green. Do not come for a tight timetable or a guaranteed glass-green Dawki boat. Families with small children or anyone who needs Nongriat stairs should prefer the drier months.
