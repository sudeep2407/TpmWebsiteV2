Living Root Bridge

Ficus roots trained across a stream — a short village walk or a long stair to Nongriat

Khasi forest bridges

Say which bridge you mean before you sell the day

Living root bridges are Ficus roots guided across water over decades. Riwai, near Mawlynnong, is a short walk and fits a first week. The double-decker at Nongriat is a long, steep stair and a night in the valley if you want to do it properly — not a tick after Nohkalikai.

Monsoon makes the steps slick and the streams loud. We will not put Nongriat on a family afternoon. Wear shoes that can take mud.

Riwai = shortNongriat = trekDry months kinder

Famous for

  • Khasi Ficus bridges grown across streams
  • Riwai for a first, short look
  • Nongriat’s double-decker for a full descent
  • A living structure, not a stone monument
  • The image Meghalaya is known for worldwide

Best time to visit

  • October to April: safer stairs and clearer forest
  • Monsoon: roots at their greenest — and most treacherous
  • Riwai: almost any dry-season day
  • Nongriat: start early, or stay in the village
