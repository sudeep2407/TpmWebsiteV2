Package Overview

The week that justifies Nongriat

Seven nights for travellers who have eight days and want the comprehensive version: Sohra’s cliffs, a dedicated double-decker day at Nongriat, the Umngot, Mawlynnong and Krang Suri. This is not a rushed sightseeing tour. The extra nights exist so the stairs are not stacked on a transfer.

Nongriat needs fitness and early starts. We will not put this card on a family that only wanted Riwai. Confirm the month — monsoon stairs are a different product.

At a glance

  • Start & end: Guwahati Airport / Railway Station
  • Stay: Shillong 2N · Sohra / Nongriat 2N · Dawki side 1N · Mawlynnong/Jaintia 2N
  • Ideal months: October–April for stairs and clear water
  • A dedicated Nongriat day — not a tick after lunch
  • Best for travellers who want the long version, not a list
