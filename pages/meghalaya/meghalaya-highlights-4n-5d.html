Meghalaya Highlights 4 Nights 5 Days

Guwahati → Shillong → Sohra → Dawki → Mawlynnong — the first Meghalaya week

Package Overview

What first-time visitors expect, without a ten-day road

Four nights for a first Meghalaya week: arrival via Umiam into Shillong, one full Sohra plateau day, then Dawki, Mawlynnong and the short Riwai root bridge before the last night back in Shillong. Pickup and drop are Guwahati Airport or Railway Station.

Seven Sisters is seasonal. Wei Sawdong is stairs, not a railing — we drop it if the group cannot do the descent. Dawki boats are extra and only worth the photograph in the clearer months.

Package Highlights

What this circuit includes

A proper Sohra day

Mawkdok, Nohkalikai, Mawsmai and Seven Sisters when the cliff is running.

Dawki on a clear-water day

Umngot boats extra — we will not sell the glass photograph in peak silt.

Riwai, not Nongriat

The short living-root walk that fits this length of trip.

Breakfast & dinner

Daily breakfast and dinner at the stay.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 01

Guwahati to Shillong

Overnight · Shillong

  • Pickup at Guwahati Airport or Railway Station.
  • Halt at Umiam Lake on the climb.
  • Shillong viewpoints if light remains.
  • Evening in town.
Day 02

Shillong sightseeing

Overnight · Shillong

  • Shillong Peak and Elephant Falls.
  • Ward’s Lake.
  • Local markets and a free evening.
Day 03

Shillong to Sohra

Overnight · Sohra

  • Mawkdok Dympep Valley on the highway.
  • Wei Sawdong Falls — stairs, dropped if the group cannot manage them.
  • Nohkalikai Falls and Mawsmai Cave.
  • Seven Sisters Falls, subject to seasonal visibility.
Day 04

Sohra to Dawki, Mawlynnong, Shillong

Overnight · Shillong

  • Dawki and the Umngot — boat extra.
  • Mawlynnong village lanes.
  • Riwai living root bridge — the short walk.
  • Return to Shillong.
Day 05

Shillong to Guwahati

Transfer · Airport / station

  • Breakfast and drive to Guwahati for departure.
