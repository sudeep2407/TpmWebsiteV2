Package Overview

The premium scenic circuit — still not Nongriat

Six nights so Sohra is two days, the Umngot gets an evening, and Krang Suri is not jammed onto the Dawki transfer. This is the week we sell when someone wants most of the famous photographs without treating the double-decker as a same-day dash.

Nongriat lives on the 7N/8D Complete Circuit. Boats and skywalk remain extra.

Premium scenicTwo Sohra daysRiver + Jaintia

At a glance

  • Start & end: Guwahati Airport / Railway Station
  • Stay: Shillong 2N · Sohra 2N · Shnongpdeng 1N · Jaintia 1N
  • Ideal months: October–April
  • Private cab for the full loop
  • Best for couples and photographers who want slack
Package Highlights

What this circuit includes

Two Sohra days

One for the main railings, one for extra falls, viewpoints and caves.

A Dawki evening

Shnongpdeng overnight after the boat.

Mawlynnong into Jaintia

Village and Krang Suri without stacking both into one exhausted afternoon from Sohra.

Breakfast & dinner

Daily breakfast and dinner at the stay.

Good to Know

Inclusions & exclusions

Included

  • Accommodation as per the night plan
  • Daily breakfast and dinner
  • Private cab for pickup drop and inter-city drives

Not included

  • Airfare or trains to Guwahati

  • Any cost from weather delays or road closures
