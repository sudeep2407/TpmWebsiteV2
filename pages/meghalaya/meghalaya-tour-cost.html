Meghalaya Tour Cost

Nights, the cab and whether Nongriat is on the card move the figure more than a headline

On this page

Cost by circuit What sits in a package Cab and group size Extras to budget Which week is good value
Meghalaya Travel Guide

What a Meghalaya week actually costs

A short Shillong–Sohra–Dawki loop is one number. Nongriat plus Jaintia is another, because the nights and the road both grow.

A practical private-tour floor is about ₹18,000–₹40,000+ per person, land only. Flights or trains to Guwahati sit outside that band.

Indicative land-package ranges

TourDurationApprox. per person*
Meghalaya Highlights4N/5D₹18,000 – ₹25,000
Meghalaya Explorer5N/6D₹21,000 – ₹29,000
Waterfalls & Caves5N/6D₹22,000 – ₹30,000
Meghalaya Scenic Escape6N/7D₹25,000 – ₹34,000
Complete Meghalaya Circuit7N/8D₹29,000 – ₹40,000+

*Comfortable private tour with stay, breakfast/dinner and the circuit cab. Dates, hotels, vehicle and occupancy change the number.

Usually included — and usually not

Stay, breakfast, dinner, private vehicle, Guwahati pickup and drop, listed sightseeing, fuel, driver, tolls and parking. Always read the card.

Common extras: lunch, flights or trains, personal spend, Umngot boats, cave and skywalk tickets, Nongriat guide or porter, heaters, laundry, insurance, and any night added because a road closed.

Why the cab dominates

A sedan suits two or three people on Highlights. An SUV suits families and the longer Scenic or Complete weeks. A tempo traveller costs more in total and less per head when six or eight share it.

Two people split the same car two ways. Four people split it four ways. A riverside night at Shnongpdeng can move the hotel line on Explorer and Scenic Escape. Monsoon does not automatically make the package cheaper — it makes the day less predictable.

Peak weeks and couple floors

Summer holidays, long weekends, Christmas and festivals push rooms and cabs. Shoulder weeks after the monsoon are often kinder.

Working land-package floors for two people on standard stays: 4N/5D about ₹36,000–₹50,000 · 5N/6D ₹42,000–₹58,000 · 6N/7D ₹50,000–₹68,000 · 7N/8D ₹58,000–₹80,000+. Tickets and boats extra. Upgrade one or two nights rather than every hotel.

Which circuit is good value?

  • Best short budget: Highlights 4N/5D
  • Best family mix: Explorer 5N/6D
  • Best nature loop: Waterfalls & Caves 5N/6D
  • Best couples week: Scenic Escape 6N/7D
  • Best complete week: Complete Circuit 7N/8D

Do not load four nights with Nongriat, Dawki and Jaintia. Highlights is better value than an overloaded short card. A 5N/6D or 6N/7D week is the usual balance.
