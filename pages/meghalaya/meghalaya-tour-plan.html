Meghalaya Tour Plan

Start in Guwahati. Choose the week by how many nights you actually have.

Meghalaya Travel Guide

A road week, not a statewide dash

Shillong is the base. Sohra is the cliff. Dawki is the river. Jaintia and Nongriat are extras that need their own nights.

Trying to see every fall in four days is mostly driving. We sell five plans. Pickup and drop: Guwahati Airport or Railway Station.

The classic loop

Highlights — 4N/5D

The first-timer card. Umiam on the way in, a Shillong town day, one Sohra plateau day, then Dawki, Mawlynnong and Riwai before the last Shillong night. Seven Sisters is seasonal. Wei Sawdong is stairs.

Explorer — 5N/6D

The same loop with a night at Shnongpdeng or Dawki so the Umngot is an evening, not a drive-through. Still not Nongriat.

The other three weeks

Waterfalls & Caves — 5N/6D

Deliberately not another Dawki card. Sohra plus Mawsynram plus Krang Suri. Use it when the river will be brown, or when the guest has already done the glass-boat photograph.

Scenic Escape — 6N/7D

Two Sohra nights, a river night, then Mawlynnong into Jaintia. The couple’s week. Still no double-decker.

Complete Circuit — 7N/8D

The only card with a dedicated Nongriat day. Stairs, early start, fitness required. If the group only wanted Riwai, sell Scenic Escape instead.

How many days are enough?

4–5 daysShillong + Sohra + Dawki + Mawlynnong
5–6 daysAdd a river night or drop Dawki for Jaintia
6–7 daysTwo Sohra days and Krang Suri
7–8 daysOnly then add Nongriat properly

Four nights is not enough for the double-decker plus Dawki plus Jaintia. Pick one extras list, not all three.

Where the week starts

Guwahati for almost every guest — flight or train. Season notes: Best Time to Visit Meghalaya. Budget notes: Meghalaya Tour Cost.
