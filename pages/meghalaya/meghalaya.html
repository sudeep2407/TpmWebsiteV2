Key Places

Explore Meghalaya

Shillong

The Khasi capital on a pine ridge — the base every week starts from.

Explore Shillong

Umiam Lake

The reservoir on the Guwahati climb — a halt, not a two-night stay.

Explore Umiam

Cherrapunji / Sohra

The wet plateau — cliffs, caves and a night that beats a rushed day trip.

Explore Sohra

Nohkalikai Falls

A single drop off the Sohra cliff — a railing viewpoint, not a swim.

Explore Nohkalikai

Seven Sisters Falls

Seven lines on one cliff — full after rain, often a dry wall in winter.

Explore Seven Sisters

Mawsmai Cave

A short lit limestone walk — the cave a first week can actually do.

Explore Mawsmai

Wei Sawdong Falls

Stepped turquoise pools — stairs down, the same stairs back up.

Explore Wei Sawdong

Mawkdok Dympep Valley

The green fold on the Sohra highway — ten honest minutes for the view.

Explore Mawkdok

Dawki & Umngot River

Glass-green border water — only in the months the silt stays low.

Explore Dawki

Mawlynnong

A kept Khasi village and bamboo skywalk — pair it with Dawki.

Explore Mawlynnong

Living Root Bridge

Riwai for a short look, Nongriat if the stairs are the point.

Explore Root Bridges

Krang Suri Falls

A Jaintia turquoise pool — its own half-day, not a Sohra add-on.

Explore Krang Suri
