Seven Sisters Falls

Seven lines on one cliff — full after rain, often a dry wall in winter

Sohra / Mawsmai cliff

A monsoon photograph, a winter geology lesson

Nohsngithiang — printed as Seven Sisters — is a set of streams that share one cliff face on the Sohra side. After heavy rain they run together as the postcard. In the dry months you may see stained rock and one or two thin lines.

The viewpoint is close to the Shillong–Sohra road. Do not promise seven white columns in January. Pair with Nohkalikai on the same loop.

Seasonal volumeRoadside viewpointBest after rain

Famous for

  • Seven streams on a single escarpment
  • A roadside halt on the Sohra drive
  • Full voice in the wet months
  • A winter cliff when the rain has stopped
  • The Khasi name Nohsngithiang

Best time to visit

  • June to October: the weeks the seven lines exist
  • November to May: come for the wall, not the postcard
  • Just after a wet night: the best dry-season chance
  • Sunset slots: popular and often crowded
