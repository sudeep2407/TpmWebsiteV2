Gangtok

Sikkim’s capital — a hillside town of monasteries, viewpoints and a car-free high street

East Sikkim

The hillside capital of Sikkim

Gangtok is Sikkim’s capital and main town, built along a ridge at about 1,650 metres. Most visitors arrive from Bagdogra Airport or New Jalpaiguri (NJP) after a four-to-five-hour drive through the Teesta valley. The town is compact: hotels, cafés and shops cluster around MG Marg, a pedestrian street that is the centre of evening life.

Around Gangtok you can visit Enchey Monastery, Rumtek Monastery a short drive away, Ganesh Tok and Tashi View Point for mountain views, and the Flower Exhibition Centre. Tsomgo Lake and Nathula Pass are organised as a separate permit day from here. Gangtok is also the starting point for journeys into North Sikkim, West Sikkim and the Silk Route.

Famous for

  • MG Marg, the car-free high street with cafés and shops
  • Rumtek and Enchey monasteries
  • Ridge viewpoints such as Ganesh Tok and Tashi View Point
  • Gateway for Tsomgo Lake, Nathula and North Sikkim
  • A walkable town with hotels at many budgets

Best time to visit

  • October to December: clear skies, pleasant days, festival season
  • March to May: mild weather and spring blossom on the hills
  • January to February: cold in town; snow is more common on the higher roads
  • June to September: monsoon — the town stays open but views and hill roads suffer
How the year feels

Gangtok through the seasons

Autumn

Crisp air and some of the year’s clearest mountain views.

Winter

Cold evenings on the ridge; town life continues as usual.

Spring

Mild days and flowering slopes around the capital.

Monsoon

Heavy rain, mist and slower journeys in and out of town.
