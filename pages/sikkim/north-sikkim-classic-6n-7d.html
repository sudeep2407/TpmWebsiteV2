Package Overview

The classic north week from Gangtok

Six nights for the usual first North Sikkim circuit: two nights in Gangtok to start and finish, one night in Lachen for Gurudongmar, and two nights in Lachung for Yumthang Valley. Pickup and drop are at New Jalpaiguri (NJP). Bagdogra can be arranged on the same transfer.

Kala Patthar, Zero Point and Mt. Katao sit outside the package and need extra permits and cost when the road is open. Gurudongmar is an early, high morning. This week is best from May to June and October to November.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 7

Scroll the journey. The car moves with you along the route — tap a day marker to jump ahead.

Day 01

NJP to Gangtok

Overnight · Gangtok

  • Pickup from NJP Railway Station (Bagdogra on request) and drive to Gangtok.
  • Check in to the hotel. Lunch and dinner at the Gangtok hotel.
  • Evening free on MG Marg if arrival time allows.
Day 02

Gangtok to Lachen

Overnight · Lachen

  • After breakfast, check out and drive toward Lachen.
  • Lunch on the way.
  • Check in at Lachen. Dinner and overnight at the Lachen hotel.
Day 03

Gurudongmar Lake, then Lachung

Overnight · Lachung

  • Early start for Shree Gurudongmar Lake.
  • Kala Patthar is available at extra cost and is not part of the package.
  • Return to Lachen for lunch, then drive to Lachung.
  • Check in. Dinner and overnight at the Lachung hotel.
Day 04

Yumthang Valley

Overnight · Lachung

  • After breakfast, drive to Yumthang Valley for sightseeing.
  • Zero Point and Mt. Katao can be added at extra cost, subject to permit and road.
  • Return to the hotel for lunch, dinner and a free evening.
Day 05

Lachung to Gangtok

Overnight · Gangtok

  • After breakfast, check out from Lachung and drive back to Gangtok.
  • Check in on arrival. Lunch and dinner at the Gangtok hotel.
Day 06

Gangtok local sightseeing

Overnight · Gangtok

  • After breakfast, a Gangtok local day. Typical stops: Ganesh Tok, Flower Exhibition Centre, Banjhakri Falls, Tashi View Point, Gonjang Monastery, the Ropeway and Sintang Monastery (also written Sutan).
  • Lunch and dinner at the Gangtok hotel.
Day 07

Gangtok to NJP

Transfer · NJP / Bagdogra

  • After breakfast, check out from Gangtok.
  • Drop at NJP Railway Station (or Bagdogra Airport) for the onward journey.
