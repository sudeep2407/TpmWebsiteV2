Pelling Ravangla Classic 4 Nights 5 Days

NJP → Pelling → Ravangla → Namchi → NJP

4 Nights 5 Days Pelling · Ravangla · Namchi Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Private Transfers
Request This Package WhatsApp Quote
Package Overview

A short west-and-south Sikkim week

Four nights when time is limited: two nights in Pelling for the Kangchenjunga ridge, monasteries, skywalk and west-side falls, then two nights in Ravangla for Buddha Park, Ralang, Namchi Char Dham, Temi Tea Garden and Samdruptse.

Pickup and drop are at NJP. Three meals a day are included. Entry fees, syndicate charges and activity rides are extra.

Short circuitWest & SouthNJP pickup

At a glance

  • Start & end: NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport
  • Stay: Pelling 2N · Ravangla 2N
  • Ideal months: October–May
  • Private cab for pickup, drop and sightseeing
  • Best for a first short Sikkim holiday without North Sikkim
Package Highlights

What this circuit includes

Pelling days

Skywalk, Pemayangtse, Rabdentse, Khecheopalri and the west falls.

Buddha Park

Ralang Monastery and Ravangla’s seated Buddha.

Namchi

Char Dham and Samdruptse on the south loop.

Temi Tea Garden

A working tea estate on the Ravangla–Namchi road.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 5

Day 01

NJP to Pelling

Overnight · Pelling

  • Transfer from NJP to Pelling.
  • Lunch and dinner at the Pelling hotel.
  • Overnight in Pelling.
Day 02

Pelling sightseeing

Overnight · Pelling

  • After breakfast, a Pelling local day. Typical stops: Sky Walk, Rimbi Waterfall, Orange Garden, Pemayangtse Monastery, bird sanctuary, Rabdentse Ruins, Kanchenjunga Falls and Khecheopalri Lake.
  • Lunch and dinner at the Pelling hotel.
Day 03

Pelling to Ravangla

Overnight · Ravangla

  • After breakfast, check out and drive to Ravangla.
  • En route: Ralang Monastery, Titanic View Point and Buddha Park.
  • Lunch and dinner at the Ravangla hotel.
Day 04

Namchi, Temi and Samdruptse

Overnight · Ravangla

  • After breakfast: Namchi Char Dham, Temi Tea Garden and Samdruptse Monastery.
  • Lunch and dinner at the Ravangla hotel.
Day 05

Ravangla to NJP

Transfer · NJP

  • After breakfast, check out from Ravangla.
  • Transfer to NJP for the onward journey.
