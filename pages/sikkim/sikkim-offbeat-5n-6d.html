Package Overview

West and south Sikkim without the north road

Five nights for a quieter family circuit: a village night at Chayatal, two nights in Pelling for Kangchenjunga and the west-side lakes and falls, one night in Yuksom — Sikkim’s first capital — and a last night in Borong after Ravangla’s Buddha Park and Temi Tea Garden.

Pickup and drop are at NJP or Bagdogra. Meals on this card are breakfast and dinner. Entry tickets, syndicate charges and any rides sit outside the package.

West & South SikkimFamily circuitNJP / Bagdogra

At a glance

  • Start & end: NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport
  • Stay: Chayatal 1N · Pelling 2N · Yuksom 1N · Borong 1N
  • Ideal months: October–May
  • Private cab for pickup, drop and sightseeing
  • Best for families who want west Sikkim over North Sikkim
Package Highlights

What this circuit includes

Pelling views

Skywalk, Chenrezig statue and Kangchenjunga on a clear morning.

Yuksom

Coronation Throne, Dubdi and Karthok monasteries.

Temi & Ravangla

Tea garden, Ralang Monastery and Buddha Park on the way to Borong.

Village nights

Chayatal and Borong homestay-style stays.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 6

Scroll the journey. The car moves with you along the route — tap a day marker to jump ahead.

Day 01

NJP / Bagdogra to Chayatal

Overnight · Chayatal

  • Pickup from NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport.
  • Scenic drive to Chayatal and check in at the homestay.
  • Evening at leisure with mountain and village views.
Day 02

Chayatal to Pelling

Overnight · Pelling

  • After breakfast, drive toward Pelling.
  • En route: Singshore Bridge, Rabdentse Ruins, the bird sanctuary and Pemayangtse Monastery.
  • Check in at Pelling.
Day 03

Pelling local sightseeing

Overnight · Pelling

  • Sky Walk and Chenrezig Statue.
  • Khecheopalri Lake.
  • Rimbi Waterfall and Kanchenjunga Falls.
Day 04

Pelling to Yuksom

Overnight · Yuksom

  • Drive to Yuksom.
  • Visit the Coronation Throne, Dubdi Monastery and Karthok Monastery.
  • Overnight in Yuksom.
Day 05

Yuksom to Borong

Overnight · Borong

  • Drive toward Borong.
  • En route: Ralang Monastery, Buddha Park at Ravangla and Temi Tea Garden.
  • Overnight at Borong.
Day 06

Borong to NJP / Bagdogra

Transfer · NJP / Bagdogra

  • After breakfast, check out from the Borong stay.
  • Transfer to NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport.
  • Tour ends.
