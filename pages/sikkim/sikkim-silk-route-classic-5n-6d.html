Package Overview

The Old Silk Route from the foothills to Zuluk

Five nights along the eastern ridges: Sillery Gaon, a riverside night at Rishikhola, Aritar, then Zuluk on the hairpin road. The last driving day crosses Thambi, Gnathang, Kupup and Tsomgo before Gangtok. Nathula Pass is extra and subject to permit.

Guests should carry a voter ID and two passport-size photographs. Children need a birth certificate. Cab permit charges are extra.

At a glance

  • Start & end: NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport
  • Stay: Sillery Gaon 1N · Rishikhola 1N · Aritar 1N · Zuluk 1N · Gangtok 1N
  • Ideal months: October–May
  • Private cab for the full NJP–NJP circuit
  • Best for travellers who want the Silk Route, not North Sikkim

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 6

Scroll the journey. The car moves with you along the route — tap a day marker to jump ahead.

Day 01

NJP to Sillery Gaon

Overnight · Sillery Gaon

  • Pickup from NJP and transfer to Sillery Gaon.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at Sillery Gaon.
  • Overnight at Sillery Gaon.
Day 02

Sillery Gaon to Rishikhola

Overnight · Rishikhola

  • After breakfast, check out and drive to Rishikhola.
  • Time by the river and surrounding forest.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at Rishikhola.
Day 03

Rishikhola to Aritar

Overnight · Aritar

  • After breakfast, check out from Rishikhola.
  • Visit Riddhi Vinayak Temple, then continue to Aritar.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at Aritar.
Day 04

Aritar to Zuluk

Overnight · Zuluk

  • After breakfast, drive toward Zuluk.
  • En route: Aritar Lake, Mankhim Viewpoint, Aritar Monastery, Quekhola Falls and the eco pine forest.
  • Lunch, evening snacks and dinner at Zuluk.
Day 05

Zuluk to Gangtok via the high road

Overnight · Gangtok

  • After breakfast, check out and drive toward Gangtok.
  • En route sightseeing typically includes Thambi View Point, Lungthung, Old Baba Mandir, Gnathang Valley, Kupup Lake, Tsomgo Lake and New Baba Mandir.
  • Nathula Pass is excluded and subject to permit.
  • Lunch and dinner at the Gangtok hotel.
Day 06

Gangtok to NJP

Transfer · NJP

  • After breakfast, check out from Gangtok.
  • Drop at NJP for the onward journey.
