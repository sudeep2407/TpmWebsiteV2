Zuluk

A high village on Sikkim’s Old Silk Route, known for its winding mountain road

East Sikkim

A village on the Old Silk Route

Zuluk (Dzuluk) is a small settlement in East Sikkim on the historic Silk Route that once linked the plains with the Tibetan plateau. It sits at about 2,900 metres. The road below the village folds into dozens of hairpin bends, best seen from Thambi Viewpoint on a clear morning. Visitors need an inner-line permit for this stretch.

Nights are spent in simple lodges. Sunrise over the eastern ridges and, in winter, snow on the loops are the main draws. Nearby Nathang Valley is a high, open pasture often combined with Zuluk. The route is different from the Tsomgo–Nathula road and is usually travelled between October and May.

Altitude ~2,900 mOld Silk RoutePermit required

Famous for

  • Hairpin bends of the Old Silk Route
  • Thambi Viewpoint over the mountain road
  • Sunrise across the eastern Himalayan ridges
  • Winter snow on the loops
  • Access to Nathang Valley nearby

Best time to visit

  • October to December: clear views and comfortable days
  • January to February: snow on the road; confirm access before travel
  • March to May: milder weather and greener slopes
  • June to September: monsoon cloud and landslides are common
How the year feels

Zuluk through the seasons

Autumn

Clear light on the hairpins after the rains.

Winter

Snow-covered loops when the road remains open.

Spring

Milder days and some blossom on the hillsides.

Monsoon

Mist and unstable roads make travel difficult.
