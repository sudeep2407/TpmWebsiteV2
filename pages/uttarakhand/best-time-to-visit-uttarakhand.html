Best Time to Visit Uttarakhand

The month depends on whether you want hill-station air, a clear Kumaon wall, a forest morning or winter cold

On this page

Seasons at a glance Spring & summer Monsoon Autumn & winter By destination By travel style
Uttarakhand Travel Guide

Uttarakhand is open all year — the week is not the same

Mussoorie and Nainital, the Ramganga forest, Binsar, Chaukori, Munsiyari and Chopta do not share one “perfect month”.

For the circuits we actually run, March to June and September to November are the most versatile. Summer fills the hill stations. Autumn clears the Kumaon wall. Winter is for cold and possible snow. Monsoon turns the state green and makes long roads less honest.

When should you go?

SeasonMonthsBest for
SpringMarch – AprilSightseeing, flowers, thinner crowds than May
SummerMay – JuneHill stations, families, escaping the plains
MonsoonJuly – AugustGreen stays — not packed road circuits
AutumnSeptember – NovemberClear peaks, photographs, Kumaon roads
WinterDecember – FebruaryCold, quiet nights, possible snow higher up

Spring and summer

March–April is comfortable in the hills and good for covering more than one halt without May’s school-holiday crush. Mussoorie, Nainital, Rishikesh, Binsar, Chopta, Chaukori, Munsiyari and Corbett all work.

May–June is the conventional family window. Higher towns stay cooler than the plains. Rishikesh and Haridwar run warmer and sit better next to Mussoorie than as a heat-week on their own. Corbett is still bookable; the terai gets hot as June goes on. Book Mussoorie and Nainital early on holiday weekends.

July and August

The state turns green. Falls run harder. Long Kumaon weeks toward Chaukori and Munsiyari need slack — rain, slips and slow roads. Mussoorie, Rishikesh, Nainital and Binsar can still be a stay-focused week. Distant peaks are often missing. Choose this season for greenery and quieter rooms, not a packed sightseeing list.

Autumn and winter

September to November is, for many people, the best Kumaon window. Skies open after the rain. Binsar, Chaukori and Munsiyari earn the drive. October and November are the months we recommend if the wall of peaks is the reason. Corbett, Chopta, Mussoorie and Rishikesh all sit comfortably here.

December to February is a different holiday — cold ridges, possible snow on Mussoorie and higher Kumaon, quieter rooms. Corbett pairs well with Nainital in the open safari months. Couples who like wood-stove nights do well; families who want easy lake weather usually pick spring or autumn instead.

Best window by place

PlaceStrongest months
Mussoorie / NainitalMarch–June · September–November
RishikeshSeptember–June (rafting off in peak monsoon)
HaridwarYear-round as a short aarti halt
Jim CorbettWinter into early summer — check zone status
BinsarMarch–June · October–December
Chaukori / Munsiyari / ChoptaMarch–June · September/October–November

By travel style

Families

  • April–June is the easy default
  • September–November if you want fewer people

Couples

  • March–April or October–November
  • Winter if cold rooms are the point

Kumaon views

  • October–November first
  • March–June if the road is the constraint

Nainital + Corbett

  • October–June while the park is working

Best overall: March–June and September–November. For how to shape the days, use the Uttarakhand Tour Plan. For land-package bands, use Uttarakhand Tour Cost.
