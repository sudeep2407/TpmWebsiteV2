Package Overview

Three Mussoorie nights, then the river, then Chopta

Seven nights for couples, younger travellers and anyone who wants nature and a little adventure without making Tungnath compulsory. Mussoorie gets a Dhanaulti option. Rishikesh keeps a rafting day. Chopta is a meadow night, not a forced trek.

Pickup and drop: Jolly Grant or Haridwar. Trekking at Chopta stays optional. Best outside the heavy monsoon.

Couples & natureOptional raftingChopta without a forced trek

At a glance

  • Start & end: Jolly Grant Airport / Haridwar Railway Station
  • Stay: Mussoorie 3N · Rishikesh 2N · Chopta 2N
  • Ideal months: April–June · September–November
  • Private cab for the full loop
  • Best for couples, young travellers, nature and adventure
