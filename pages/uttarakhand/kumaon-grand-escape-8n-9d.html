Package Overview

The Retreat week, then the Panchachuli road

Eight nights for couples, photographers and travellers who already know the hills a little: two nights each in Nainital, Binsar, Chaukori and Munsiyari. The Chaukori–Munsiyari drive is part of the week, not a transfer to hide.

The last day is a long return to the plains. Winter snow can close the last stretch — we confirm the road before we sell this card.

At a glance

  • Start & end: Jolly Grant Airport / Haridwar Railway Station
  • Stay: Nainital 2N · Binsar 2N · Chaukori 2N · Munsiyari 2N
  • Ideal months: April–June · October–November
  • Private cab for the full expedition
  • Best for couples, photographers, experienced hill travellers
