Package Overview

Lake, oak forest, then a tea-ridge sunrise

Six nights for couples and travellers who want quieter Kumaon: two nights each in Nainital, Binsar and Chaukori. No Corbett, no Munsiyari road. The last day is a long return to the plains.

Best when the wall is likely to show — October–November and the spring shoulder. Not a first-timer “see everything” card.

Quieter KumaonCouples & natureNo forced trek

At a glance

  • Start & end: Jolly Grant Airport / Haridwar Railway Station
  • Stay: Nainital 2N · Binsar 2N · Chaukori 2N
  • Ideal months: March–June · October–November
  • Private cab for the full loop
  • Best for couples, nature, a quieter Uttarakhand
