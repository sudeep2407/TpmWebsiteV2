Mussoorie

A ridge town above the Doon — walkable evenings, sharper views after the rains

Garhwal hill station

Two nights if you want Landour as well as the Mall

Mussoorie sits on a long ridge above Dehradun, looking north toward snow and south into the Doon. The Mall is the evening the town is known for. Landour, a little higher, is quieter — Lal Tibba, old cantonment lanes, and the original reason many people extend the stay.

Kempty Falls and Camel’s Back work as half-days from a single hotel. Weekend May crowds are real. October and November give clearer ridges with fewer rooms filled.

2–3 nightsDehradun 1 hourMall + Landour

Famous for

  • Mall Road evenings above the Doon
  • Landour and Lal Tibba for a longer view
  • Kempty Falls and the Camel’s Back walk
  • Colonial ridge town that is still a working hill station
  • A first Garhwal halt from Delhi or Dehradun

Best time to visit

  • March to June: the main comfortable window
  • September to November: clearer Himalayan light, thinner crowds
  • December to February: cold; occasional snow on the ridge
  • July–August: mist and landslide risk on the Dehradun road
How the year feels

Mussoorie through the seasons

Spring / early summer

Pleasant days, busy weekends, everything open.

Monsoon

Cloud on the Mall; the highway needs patience.

Autumn

The month many regulars prefer — sharp views, quieter lanes.

Winter

Cold walks. Snow is possible, not guaranteed.
