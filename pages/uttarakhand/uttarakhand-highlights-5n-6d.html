Package Overview

A first Uttarakhand week on the Garhwal side

Five nights for first-time visitors, couples and families: two nights on the Mussoorie ridge, two on the Ganga at Rishikesh, one at Haridwar for the aarti. Pickup and drop are Jolly Grant Airport or Haridwar Railway Station.

Rafting is optional and extra. The aarti evening in Haridwar is kept free. Best for a compact introduction — not for Chopta or Kumaon.

Package Highlights

What this circuit includes

Two Mussoorie nights

Kempty, Gun Hill, Landour and the Mall — not a same-day ridge halt.

Rishikesh river days

Jhulas, Beatles Ashram, optional rafting, evening aarti.

A Haridwar aarti night

Har Ki Pauri kept as an evening, not a drive-through.

Breakfast & dinner

Daily breakfast and dinner at the stay.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow the circuit from Day 1 to Day 6

Scroll the journey. The car moves with you along the route — tap a day marker to jump ahead.

Day 01

Arrival to Mussoorie

Overnight · Mussoorie

  • Pickup from Jolly Grant Airport or Haridwar Railway Station.
  • Drive to Mussoorie and check in.
  • Evening on Mall Road and Camel’s Back / local market.
Day 02

Mussoorie sightseeing

Overnight · Mussoorie

  • Kempty Falls and Company Garden.
  • Gun Hill.
  • Lal Tibba / Landour.
  • Mall Road in the evening.
Day 03

Mussoorie to Rishikesh

Overnight · Rishikesh

  • Breakfast and check-out.
  • Drive to Rishikesh and check in.
  • Ram Jhula area and the Ganga riverfront.
  • Evening Ganga aarti.
Day 04

Rishikesh sightseeing & adventure

Overnight · Rishikesh

  • Beatles Ashram and the Lakshman Jhula area.
  • Local cafés.
  • Optional river rafting — extra, season depending.
  • Evening at leisure.
Day 05

Rishikesh to Haridwar

Overnight · Haridwar

  • Drive to Haridwar after breakfast.
  • Har Ki Pauri and the local market.
  • Evening Ganga aarti.
Day 06

Haridwar to Jolly Grant

Transfer · Airport / station

  • Breakfast and transfer to Jolly Grant Airport for departure.
Good to Know
