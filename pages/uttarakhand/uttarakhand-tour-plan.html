Uttarakhand Tour Plan

Choose a region. Do not try to cover the whole state in one week.

Uttarakhand Travel Guide

Regional circuits, not a statewide dash

Hill stations, the Ganga, Corbett and the quieter Kumaon line do not belong on the same six-night card.

Pickup and drop on all five plans: Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun, or Haridwar Junction. Kathgodam can be discussed later for a Kumaon-only week.

Garhwal weeks

Highlights — 5N/6D

The easiest first trip. Two Mussoorie nights (Kempty, Landour, the Mall), two Rishikesh nights (jhulas, optional rafting), one Haridwar aarti night. Not Chopta. Not Kumaon.

Nature & Adventure — 7N/8D

Three Mussoorie nights with an optional Dhanaulti day, two on the Ganga, two at Chopta. Tungnath is not compulsory — the meadow night is the point. A long last run back to the plains.

Kumaon weeks

Nainital & Corbett — 5N/6D

Three lake nights including Bhimtal and Mukteshwar, then two forest nights and a jeep morning by zone. Safari is extra and never a promised tiger.

Mountain Retreat — 6N/7D

Two nights each in Nainital, Binsar and Chaukori. Oak walks and a tea-ridge sunrise. No Munsiyari road.

Grand Escape — 8N/9D

The Retreat line plus two Munsiyari nights under Panchachuli. Sell it as a long scenic holiday, not a quick list. Confirm the high road in winter before you book it.

How many days are enough?

4–5 daysOne compact region only
5–6 daysMussoorie–Rishikesh or Nainital–Corbett
6–7 daysA relaxed Kumaon or Garhwal loop
8–10 daysMunsiyari or a deeper Kumaon line

Mussoorie plus Nainital plus Corbett plus Munsiyari in one short week is mostly driving. Pick Garhwal or Kumaon.

Where the week starts

Jolly Grant if you fly. Haridwar Junction if you take the train. Season notes live in Best Time to Visit Uttarakhand. Budget notes in Uttarakhand Tour Cost.
