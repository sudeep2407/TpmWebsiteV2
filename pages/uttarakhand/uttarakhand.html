Explore Uttarakhand

Mussoorie

Ridge town above the Doon — Mall evenings and a quieter Landour.

Explore Mussoorie

Rishikesh

Ganga town of jhulas, aarti and a river that is not a backdrop.

Explore Rishikesh

Chopta

High meadow under the Kedar range — Tungnath and Chandrashila.

Explore Chopta

Haridwar

Har Ki Pauri and the evening aarti where the Ganga meets the plains.

Explore Haridwar

Nainital

The lake in a fold of seven hills — a Kumaon family base.

Explore Nainital

Binsar

Oak sanctuary above Almora and a Himalayan wall at first light.

Explore Binsar

Munsiyari

Johar valley under the Panchachuli peaks — a long Kumaon road.

Explore Munsiyari

Chaukori

Tea terraces and a quiet sunrise halt on the Pithoragarh line.

Explore Chaukori

Jim Corbett

Ramganga forest after the lake — a safari morning, never a promised tiger.

Explore Corbett
